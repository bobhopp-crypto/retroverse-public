# Creative Lab Phase 6 — Art First, Pass Second

## Goal

Replace layout-strategy concept cards with four illustrated art-direction boards. User chooses visual worlds, not business-card layouts.

## Art Directions

| Key | Direction | Visual identity |
|-----|-----------|-----------------|
| A | Psychedelic Festival | Sunburst, peace sign, ornate borders, flower-power palette |
| B | Saturday Morning Cartoon | Thick outlines, character face, Hanna-Barbera flat color |
| C | Vintage Television | CRT frame, ON AIR badge, gold studio guest plate |
| D | Collector Memorabilia | Trading-card frame, numbered edition, foil corners |

## Workflow

1. **Choose visual world** — `USE THIS DIRECTION`
2. **8 refinements** — border, typography, density, numbering, motifs (same art family)
3. **Select winner** — `USE THIS VERSION` → direction locked

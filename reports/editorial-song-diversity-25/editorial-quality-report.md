## Research and provenance

Existing packet material supplied the factual anchors for Tony Joe White, the Bee Gees, Bob Seger, Aerosmith, Dire Straits, Paul Simon, Toto, Talking Heads, Spin Doctors, Biz Markie, Los Lonely Boys, Chris Stapleton, Blanco Brown, and related songs. Ten songs had no usable Collector packet source URL. Their new passages were kept deliberately conservative and interpretive rather than adding unsupported dates, credits, quotations, or causal claims. They remain candidates for a later source-deepening pass if the editorial standard requires footnoted research for every article.

## Repetition audit

The principal limitation is length. The articles are intentionally concise, but this pass is below the requested 350–700-word range. That is a real editorial gap rather than something to hide: the architecture passes the diversity test, while the next editorial pass should deepen the strongest packets selectively instead of padding every song to one fixed length.

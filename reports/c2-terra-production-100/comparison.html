How “Brimful of Asha” Found Its Second Chart Life

Cornership’s “Brimful of Asha” had two distinct releases in 1997. The song appeared on the English alternative rock band’s third album, When I Was Born for the 7th Time, and its original Wiiija single release reached number 60 on the UK Singles Chart. That first chart position was not the end of the record’s story. Norman Cook, working as Fatboy Slim, remixed “Brimful of Asha,” and the new version became a radio and critical success. Re-released, the song returned to the UK chart with a markedly different result: number one. The remix also carried the track to number 16 on the US Billboard Modern Rock Tracks chart. For a song first issued as part of Cornershop’s 1997 album campaign, the re-release created a separate public life—one measured by a leap from a modest original UK showing to the top spot.
